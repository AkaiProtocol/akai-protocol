    FAQ

    Short answers.

    Akai, briefly?

    A Robinhood Chain project that turns holding $AKAI into cheaper access to AI tools.

    Has $AKAI launched?

    No contract address is stated yet. The slot pinned above everything stays inert until a real address is published.

    How large is the discount?

    The stated ceiling is 95% below standard pricing, bounded by a daily allowance. Actual savings track your usage.

    Does Akai touch my funds?

    Connecting a wallet lets Akai read the balance it holds. This site has no connect button and never requests a signature.

    Where do the chain figures originate?

    Height and gas arrive from the chain node. Locked value comes from DefiLlama. Pairs come from DexScreener.

    Why is a card showing a dash?

    Do listed pools carry an endorsement?

    No. Pools open freely, so treat each row as unvetted and verify it personally.
